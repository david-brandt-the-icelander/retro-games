TWELVE BANNERS

Found cities on a hex world, research from Agriculture to Space Flight, and outlast up to eleven rivals, played by friends or by the computer.

Play in the browser Download for desktop

The browser build plays against the computer and needs WebGL 2. Online games with friends, up to twelve civilizations, are in the desktop builds for now. The game is still being built: expect changes, and there are no saved games yet.

Playing together

HostChoose Host Game. The lobby shows the addresses your friends join at; over the internet, forward UDP port 24565 on your router.
JoinChoose Join Game with the host's address.
SeatsUp to twelve civilizations. The host sets how many the computer plays, and takes over for anyone who drops out.
TurnsEveryone moves at once. The turn ends when every player has pressed End Turn.

How to play

CitiesFound cities with Settlers (B). They grow on surplus food, work their best tiles and build units, buildings, Wealth or Research.
LandWorkers build farms, mines and trading posts. Borders grow when a city reaches size 3.
WarRight-click to move or attack; hover first to see the path or the odds. A city at 0 HP falls to a melee attack.
WinBe the last civilization standing, launch the Spaceship, or have the highest score on turn 300.
CameraWASD, arrow keys or the screen edge to pan, wheel to zoom, Q / E to rotate, H for your capital.
KeysEnter ends the turn, T opens the technologies, F fortifies, Space skips a unit, Tab goes to the next one.
