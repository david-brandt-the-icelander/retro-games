RALLY

Two paddles. One ball. First to eleven.

COMPUTER

HOW TO PLAY

Hit the ball past the other paddle. Meet it near your paddle's tip to send it off at a sharp angle; every return is a little faster than the last.

First to 11 wins, but you must be two points clear. The serve changes every two points, and every point from 10–10. While you hold the ball, move to choose where you serve from.
