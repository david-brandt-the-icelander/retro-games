HAND OF THE ISLE

You never command your people. You raise and lower the land beneath them, and call down earthquakes, swamps and floods, so that your followers outgrow a rival god's.

Play in the browser

Needs WebGL 2, a keyboard and a mouse with a right button. Build {{version}}.

How to play

Flat landYour people (blue) build on flat, dry ground. The more flat land at the same height around a home, the bigger it grows: hut, house, then castle. Homes on uneven ground collapse; homes under the sea drown.
FaithFull homes send out new followers, and followers give you faith. Faith pays for every power, sculpting included.
SculptLeft-click raises the land, right-click lowers it; hold to keep going. You can only sculpt near your own people, the bright part of the island.
FollowersZ settles, X gathers your people at a Beacon into one strong band, C sends them on a crusade against the rival (red).
Powers1–8: Sculpt, Beacon, Earthquake, Swamp, Champion, Volcano, Flood and Armageddon. Shift keeps a power selected after casting.
CameraW A S D or the arrow keys to pan, Q / E or middle-drag to rotate, the wheel to zoom, Home for your people.
WinThe rival god has no people left. Space pauses, F fast-forwards, H shows the controls.
