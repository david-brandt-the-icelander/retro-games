IRON HAZE

Pump crude from the oil wells, raise riveted ironclads, siege howitzers and bomber zeppelins, and break the Iron Dominion before it breaks you.

Play in the browser Download for desktop

The browser build needs WebGL 2 and a keyboard and mouse. The desktop builds add volumetric haze with sun shafts, ambient occlusion and scorch marks.

How to play

FuelSelect your Constructor and build Pump Jacks (Z) on the dark oil wells. Every structure extends how far you can build.
ArmyBarracks (X) train Gasmask Troopers; the War Factory (C) builds Ironclad Tanks and Thunderer Howitzers; the Airship Mast (V) launches Stormcloud Zeppelins. Flak Towers (B) shred airships.
WinDestroy every structure of the Iron Dominion.
CameraArrow keys or the screen edge to pan, wheel to zoom, Q / E or middle-drag to rotate.
OrdersLeft-click or drag to select; right-click to move or attack; A then click to attack-move; S to stop; Ctrl+0–9 for groups.
ManualF1 or ? in the game.
