# Signs in Backseat Driver

The web build draws 88 Icelandic road signs and uses 46 of them in 47 driving events. **This is not the complete set of signs a learner driver in Iceland must know**; see *What is not covered* below.

## Conventions used

- Warning: red-bordered triangle, yellow background, black pictogram. Prohibitory: red-ringed circle, yellow background. Mandatory: blue circle with white border. Information and special-rule signs: blue rectangle. Stop: red octagon. Give way: inverted red-bordered triangle. Priority road: yellow diamond with white edge.
- Warning and prohibitory backgrounds are yellow per the English Wikipedia article *Road signs in Iceland* and Guide to Iceland; one driving-theory site says white. The give-way interior colour and several pictograms are **not verified** against an official source.
- All pictograms are simplified redrawings made for this game, not the official artwork.
- Codes are the file codes used on Wikipedia (the pre-2024 letter scheme). Regulation 250/2024 (in force 1 March 2024) renumbers signs in a 100-series scheme; older signs stay valid until replaced. Icelandic names appear only where Samgöngustofa's page gave them.

## Sources

- [Road signs in Iceland (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_signs_in_Iceland): code list and colour conventions (read in part; the final ~⅓ came as a summary).
- [Samgöngustofa: Umferðarmerki](https://island.is/s/samgongustofa/umferdarmerki): 2024 categories and Icelandic names.
- Regulation 250/2024 itself returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching, so the authoritative sign list was not read.

## What is not covered

The brief was *every sign needed for the driving licence*. That list could not be obtained, so the following known groups are **missing** and are the next work:

- Warning: other junction variants (A07.21, A07.22, A07.3x, A07.4x), A01.2x curves, A14.21/A14.22, A11.xx variants, J34 and J39 to J43 Iceland-specific plates, 2024 warnings 108.2, 115, 149, 153.
- Priority: 212/214 as separate 2024 signs, 216 zipper merge, 455 minimum speed.
- Prohibitory: B03.11, B03.35/36, B03.71, B14, B17, B18, B19.21, B22/B29 zones, B33.21/41, B30 variants.
- Mandatory: C01.11/12/26/31/32/41, C09.12/21, C15.
- Special rule and information: lane signs (G-series), D01.x parking family, D09.11, D11, D16, D18, D20, D06.5x, speed-hump surface marking.
- Whole categories: service signs (E-series, about 100 symbols), route and direction signs (F-series), sub-signs (J-series, most), variable signs, temporary work-zone signs, traffic lights, road markings.
- No railway crossing signs are included because Iceland has no railways.

To close the gap, supply the official sign list or the driving-theory syllabus (for example a PDF of regulation 250/2024) and the missing signs can be added to `web/src/signs_c.js` and given events in `web/src/events_*.js`.
