# Player

**Kind:** person in a role
**Evidence:** `games/rally/worker/index.ts` (`AUTH`, `gamePage`: the page and the score API need a sign-in to icelander.net with the `games:play` role), `games/rally/src/main.ts` (`start`, `joinMatch`), `games/rally/src/scores.ts` (`defaultName`)
**Evidence (Gambit):** `games/chess/worker/index.ts` (`default.fetch`: the page needs a sign-in to icelander.net with the `games:play` role), `games/chess/worker/auth.ts` (`currentUser`: Gambit's own player record, keyed by address), `games/chess/worker/api.ts` (`handleApi`: an invitation is answered by the player signed in with the address it was sent to), `games/chess/godot/scripts/main.gd` (`Main._lobby`, `Main._setup`, `Main._history`)
**Evidence (Samosa):** `games/dictator/worker/index.ts` (`AUTH`, `gamePage`: the page and the score API need a sign-in to icelander.net with the `games:play` role), `games/dictator/src/main.ts` (`title`, `io`, `offerTable`), `games/dictator/src/scores.ts` (`defaultName`)
**Evidence (Hivestorm):** `games/hivestorm/worker/index.ts` (`AUTH`, `gamePage`: the same sign-in and role), `games/hivestorm/src/ui/input.ts` (`Controls`), `games/hivestorm/src/main.ts` (`step`, `joinMatch`, `offerTable`)
**Evidence (Deep Claim):** `games/deep-claim/worker/index.ts` (`gamePage`: the page needs a sign-in to icelander.net with the `games:play` role), `games/deep-claim/godot/scripts/main.gd` (`Main._update_playing`)
**Evidence (Shatterbelt):** `games/shatterbelt/worker/index.ts` (`AUTH`, `gamePage`: Shatterbelt's page and score API need the same sign-in and role), `games/shatterbelt/src/main.ts` (`launch`, `joinMatch`), `games/shatterbelt/src/input.ts` (`Input.controls`: one pilot per browser)
**Evidence (Dulmál):** `games/dulmal/worker/index.ts` (`default.fetch`: the page, the room API and each room's WebSocket need a sign-in to icelander.net with the invite-only role `games:dulmal`), `games/dulmal/src/shared/game.ts` (`join`, `claim`: a seat in a room, kept by a token in the browser), `games/dulmal/src/client/main.ts` (`connect`)
**Evidence (Kvarðinn):** `games/kvardinn/worker/handler.ts` (`handle`: the same sign-in, and the invite-only role games:kvardinn), `games/kvardinn/src/engine/game.ts` (`addPlayer`: a seat is a name, a team and a seat token; `effectiveHost`: the first player in, or while they are away the earliest-joined player online, gets the host's buttons), `games/kvardinn/src/client/net.ts` (`loadSeat`, `saveSeat`: the seat is kept in that browser)
**Evidence (Laumufarþegi):** `games/laumufarthegi/worker/index.ts` (`openSocket`: a room's WebSocket needs no sign-in, only the room's code), `games/laumufarthegi/worker/room.ts` (`Room.webSocketMessage`: a player is a seat taken by name and kept by its token), `games/laumufarthegi/shared/game.ts` (`Player`, `addPlayer`, `view`), `games/laumufarthegi/web/app.ts` (`joinScreen`, `roundScreen`, `voteCard`)
**Evidence (Lundey):** `games/lundey/worker/index.ts` (`default.fetch`: every file needs a sign-in to icelander.net with the invite-only role `games:lundey`), `games/lundey/worker/config.ts` (`ROLE`), `games/lundey/src/ui/input.ts` (`Controls`: one player's keys, swipes, buttons or gamepad), `games/lundey/src/main.ts` (`BEST_KEY`: the best score, kept in that browser)

## What they want from the system

- To play Rally against the computer, at a level they choose, and get onto the high-score table ([Rally's use cases](../games/rally/src/.dev/use-case.json), `play-and-enter-table`; [journeys](../games/rally/.dev/user-journey.json), `climb-the-table`).
- To play a friend who is on another device ([Rally's use cases](../games/rally/src/.dev/use-case.json), `play-online`; [journeys](../games/rally/.dev/user-journey.json), `play-a-friend`).
- To play a friend somewhere else at their own pace in Gambit: invite them by email, play over days and chat as they go ([Gambit's use cases](../games/chess/godot/scripts/.dev/use-case.json), `invite-a-friend`, `answer-an-invitation`, `play-online`; [journeys](../games/chess/.dev/user-journey.json), `play-a-friend`).
- To play the computer in Gambit at a strength they choose, and keep the game ([Gambit's use cases](../games/chess/godot/scripts/.dev/use-case.json), `play-the-computer`; [journeys](../games/chess/.dev/user-journey.json), `play-the-computer`).
- To look back on their Gambit games and their record against each friend ([Gambit's use cases](../games/chess/godot/scripts/.dev/use-case.json), `replay-a-game`, `visit-a-contact`; [journeys](../games/chess/.dev/user-journey.json), `relive-a-game`).
- To rule Samosa's Nicaragua for as long and as well as they can, month by month, and get out alive with money in Switzerland ([Samosa's use cases](../games/dictator/src/engine/.dev/use-case.json), `survive-a-month`; [journeys](../games/dictator/.dev/user-journey.json), `a-reign`).
- To put a reign of Samosa on its high-score table, and see it ranked ([Samosa's use cases](../games/dictator/src/.dev/use-case.json), `reign-and-enter-table`; [journeys](../games/dictator/.dev/user-journey.json), `climb-the-table`).
- To play Hivestorm against the hive and get onto its high-score table ([Hivestorm's use cases](../games/hivestorm/src/.dev/use-case.json), `play-and-enter-table`; [journeys](../games/hivestorm/.dev/user-journey.json), `climb-the-table`).
- To play a friend at Hivestorm on another device, taking turns, each with their own score on the table ([Hivestorm's use cases](../games/hivestorm/src/.dev/use-case.json), `play-online`; [journeys](../games/hivestorm/.dev/user-journey.json), `play-a-friend`).
- To fly Shatterbelt alone and get onto its high-score table ([Shatterbelt's use cases](../games/shatterbelt/src/.dev/use-case.json), `fly-solo-and-enter-table`; [journeys](../games/shatterbelt/.dev/user-journey.json), `climb-the-table`).
- To fly Shatterbelt with up to eight friends online, each on their own device, together or against each other, and see everyone's scores on the table ([Shatterbelt's use cases](../games/shatterbelt/src/.dev/use-case.json), `fly-online`; [journeys](../games/shatterbelt/.dev/user-journey.json), `fly-with-friends`).
- To play Dulmál, an Icelandic team word game, with friends and family in the same room or elsewhere, each on their own phone, by opening one shared link ([Dulmál's deployment diagram](../games/dulmal/.dev/deployment-diagram.json)).
- To play Kvarðinn with friends, some in the same room and some remote, each on their own phone, joining from one link with a name: give clues, guess on the scale as a team, and win or beat the group's best ([Kvarðinn's deployment](../games/kvardinn/.dev/deployment-diagram.json); [the phases of a game](../games/kvardinn/src/engine/.dev/Game.state-diagram.json)).
- To join a Laumufarþegi game from a link someone shared, with just a name, and play its rounds on their own phone ([Laumufarþegi's use cases](../games/laumufarthegi/web/.dev/use-case.json), `join-from-link`, `play-a-round`; [journeys](../games/laumufarthegi/.dev/user-journey.json), `join-from-a-link`).
- To keep a Laumufarþegi game going when its host has gone ([use cases](../games/laumufarthegi/web/.dev/use-case.json), `take-over-as-host`).
- To play Lundey alone: gather the sand eels of one burrow after another, turn on the hunters with a tern's feather, and beat the best score kept on their own device ([Lundey's use cases](../games/lundey/src/.dev/use-case.json), `play-a-game`; [journeys](../games/lundey/.dev/user-journey.json), `a-run-at-the-best`).

## How they reach it

Rally's page at icelander.net/games/retro/rally/, signed in to icelander.net, with a keyboard, a mouse or a touch screen. Online matches start on the games hub's multiplayer page, which opens the game with `?match=ID`; the other player in an online match is a player too.

Gambit is at icelander.net/games/retro/chess/, signed in to icelander.net, with a mouse or a touch screen. An invitation's link (`?challenge=ID`) opens it at that invitation; the friend invited is a player too, known by the email address the invitation was sent to.

Samosa's page at icelander.net/games/retro/samosa/, signed in to icelander.net, with a keyboard, or on a touch screen with the keypad of the keys each prompt accepts; its scores page needs no sign-in.

Hivestorm's page at icelander.net/games/retro/hivestorm/, signed in to icelander.net, with a keyboard or the touch buttons under the screen. Its online games also start on the multiplayer page, which opens the game with `?match=ID`; there, the other player plays their own turns of the same two-player game.

Shatterbelt's page is at icelander.net/games/retro/shatterbelt/, signed in the same way, with a keyboard, a gamepad or a touch screen; its online games, for two to nine pilots, start on the same multiplayer page, and every pilot in them is a player too.

Dulmál's page is at icelander.net/games/dulmal/, signed in to icelander.net with the invite-only role `games:dulmal`: a player opens a room's link (`?herbergi=CODE`), or types its four-letter code, and gives a name. A TV or projector in the room can show the board with `&skjar=1`; it is a screen, not a player.
Kvarðinn is at icelander.net/games/kvardinn/, signed in to icelander.net with the invite-only role games:kvardinn (a standalone copy on a workers.dev address needs no sign-in): a player opens the room's link or types its code, gives a name and picks a team, on a phone or a laptop. The host is a player too, the first one in; the code gives them more buttons but knows them only as a seat. A big screen (`#CODE/skjar`) is a device the players open, not a player: it holds no seat.
Laumufarþegi's page is at icelander.net/games/laumufarthegi/, opened from a room's link (`?r=CODE`) or by typing the room's code, on a phone or a computer. Unlike the other games, a player needs no sign-in: only the [Room host](room-host.md) who starts a room signs in, with the invite-only role, and a player is known only by the name they give and the seat token their browser keeps.

Lundey's page is at icelander.net/games/lundey/, signed in to icelander.net with the invite-only role `games:lundey`, with a keyboard, with swipes or the on-screen buttons on a touch screen, or with a gamepad. One player per device; the game knows nothing of the player but the best score their browser keeps. It has not been deployed yet ([what it waits for](../games/lundey/.dev/blockers.json), `first-deploy`).
