LOADING
Type LOAD "" and press ENTER, then start the tape. The game loads in about
three minutes.

THE STORY
For forty years old Ezra Thimble kept Lock Seven on the Marrowdeep Cut. Now
Ezra has gone to live with his sister in the hills, and the Pellandine
Navigation Company has handed you his windlass, his keys and his kettle.

Every morning a boat noses up to your gates. Let it through and the boatmen
pay their toll, but each lockful of water you let down drains the pound above
you. Turn a boat away and you keep your water, but word travels fast along the
towpath and your good name suffers. Only the rain can fill the pound again.

PLAYING THE GAME
Press O to open the gates or K to keep them shut. The Company inspector pays
nothing: he charges you a fee to look at your lock, though the boatmen think
better of you for letting him through.

You lose your post if the pound runs dry, if your purse is emptied, or if the
boatmen lose all patience with you. Keep the lock for twenty days and the
Company will pension you off to a cottage by the water.

HINTS
A wise keeper watches the sky. Coal pays best but drinks the most water.
